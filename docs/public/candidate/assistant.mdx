# Use the assistant

Some Exams offer an AI assistant in Proctor Desktop. If yours does, the
assistant panel lets you ask it questions during your Attempt. Your Exam
Manager decides how much it may help, and Desktop shows you which model you
are talking to. If your Exam doesn't offer it, there is no panel.

<Maturity state="preview">
  The server and the API support the assistant. The Desktop app's code isn't
  in this repository, so this page describes what the assistant does without
  guessing at the exact labels on Desktop's screens.
</Maturity>

## What it can do

Your Exam sets one help level:

| Level | What the assistant does |
| --- | --- |
| Tutor | Explains concepts, the language and its libraries. It can't see your files. |
| Reviewer | Also reads your files and, if your Exam has a terminal, its screen. It explains errors and gives hints, but doesn't write your code. |
| Pair | Also suggests changes as a diff. Nothing changes until you apply it yourself. If your Exam enables execution, it can run commands and start interactive sessions. |
| Agent | Also changes your files and runs commands itself, as your Exam allows. |

The assistant can only see what you can see in this Attempt. It never types
into your terminal: its commands run separately, and their changes are
marked as the assistant's.

Some replies may be shortened to a hint. Your Exam Manager can ask the
assistant to keep back certain topics or long pieces of code, and Proctor
checks replies before you see them.

## It asks before it acts

Unless your Exam says otherwise, the assistant asks you before it runs a
command, starts a session, types into one, or changes a file. You see
the command, the exact text and keys, or the diff, and can accept or decline.
When your Exam requires approval, each new input has its own request. If
you don't answer within 5 minutes, the request is declined.

You can stop a reply at any time, including a command that runs as part
of it. Interactive sessions stay open across replies.

### Watch or stop an interactive session

An interactive session lets the assistant try a program that waits for
input. You can watch its screen and stop the session. Its screen is
read-only. To type into the program yourself, open it in your own terminal.
The assistant can have two sessions open at once. Submitting your Attempt
ends any sessions that are still open.

## Limits and outages

Your Exam limits how much you can use the assistant, for example how many
messages you can send. When a limit is reached, the assistant tells you and
stops. If the assistant is unavailable, it says so. Either way, your Exam
carries on: the assistant never stops you working or submitting. You can
only have one reply in progress at a time.

## You can read your whole conversation

Everything you and the assistant say, and everything it reads, runs or
changes, is recorded with your Attempt. You can read your own conversation
during the Attempt. After submission, you can download the sealed transcript
as JSON through `GET /api/v1/exam-attempts/{exam_attempt_id}/assistant/export`.
The download contains the digest from your Submission receipt and remains
available until the transcript is retired.

Your Exam Managers read the same record when they review your Submission.
Using the assistant in an Exam that offers it isn't misconduct, and Proctor
raises no integrity flags for it.

## What leaves Proctor

The assistant sends your messages, and at Reviewer and above the files and
output it reads, to the model provider your institution chose. By default,
Proctor doesn't tell the model your name, email address or ID. It can't remove
personal details you write yourself, so keep them out of your code and your
messages.
