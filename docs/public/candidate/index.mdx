# Take an Exam with Proctor Desktop

You can only take an Exam in Proctor Desktop. Your copy of Desktop must be a
compatible version, and it must have a Session tied to its registered key. You
can't start an Attempt from an ordinary browser Session or with a copied
bearer token.

## Your Exam, step by step

1. [Prepare and enter](./prepare-and-enter.mdx) from Candidate Exam Activity,
   once Desktop has passed its compatibility check and you have authorized it.
2. [Keep your Workspace and Participation up to date](./workspace-and-continuity.mdx)
   while Desktop confirms that the server has saved every change.
3. [Learn how the Exam browser works and what it records](./browser-and-privacy.mdx)
   before you follow links to outside reference material.
4. [Submit, or get going again](./submit-and-recover.mdx) after a correction,
   a pause, a disconnection, a Suspension or a sync conflict.
5. [Read your result once it has been released](./released-result.mdx). The
   result doesn't show private evidence, decisions or manager notes.

## What the status on your Exam card means

An Exam Activity card helps you find your way to the right Exam. It is not a
lasting promise that you will be let in. A card can show a Sitting as
upcoming, available, in progress, submitted, result available or past. It
offers only the actions that are safe right now, such as enter, resume or view
result.

When you enter, Proctor checks everything again: your Class membership, the
state of the Sitting, whether your Desktop build is compatible, your Desktop
Registration, and your current Session.

Proctor delivers programming Exams securely and records signals about exam
integrity. It doesn't decide your grade, score, rubric, pass or fail, or
whether you did anything wrong.
