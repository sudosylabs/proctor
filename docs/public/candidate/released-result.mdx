# Read your released result

Submitting your Attempt doesn't publish an integrity Review straight away. An
authorized Exam Manager first has to record every required non-academic
decision, finalize the Review, and then choose to release your result.

Until then, Proctor answers as if the result doesn't exist ("not found"). That
way, nothing about an unreleased Review can slip out through its status, its
timing or part of its content.

Once your result is released, Proctor shows you only the remarks approved for
students, along with the identifiers of the Review, the Submission and the
Attempt, and when the result was released. It doesn't show:

- Integrity Flags, evidence or discrepancies;
- manager decisions, private reasons or private notes;
- the sealed Submission's file list or file contents;
- a grade, score, rubric, pass or fail, or any other academic outcome.

An integrity decision isn't a finding that you did something wrong, and it
isn't a grade. Your Institution is in charge of any academic process beyond
this short result in Proctor.

If you were told a result is ready but you can't see it, check that you are
signed in with the same account and Institution you used for the Attempt. If
you still can't see it, contact your Institution. Don't ask support staff to
send you private Review content by email or chat.
